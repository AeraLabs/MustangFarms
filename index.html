✳ GET TO KNOW US

Meet Ed.
Our farmer,
our friend.

After 22 years serving in the military, Ed Sperry started a whole new adventure: farming.

He didn't grow up knowing all the tricks of the trade. He started learning, asking questions, and watching YouTube videos. What began as a leap into the unknown is becoming a little farm rooted in hard work and the joy of growing something good.

Before you

Can I place a real order yet?

Not just yet. This is a preview of the farmstand. You can try a test pickup request with sample details and see it appear at the Farmer's Desk in this tab. It won't be sent to Ed.

Where is the farm?

Mustang Farms is in Meridian, Idaho. Confirmed pickup directions and hours will be added before real orders open.

What's available each season?

A small garden changes with the weather and the season. The produce and prices here are examples. Ed's actual harvest will be listed when the farmstand opens.

Do I pay online?

No payment is collected in this preview. Payment and pickup arrangements will be confirmed before the farmstand opens for real orders.
